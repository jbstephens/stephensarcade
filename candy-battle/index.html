What is Candy Battle?
Candy Battle turns match-3 into a duel: every candy you clear powers your side of the fight. Chain matches, charge attacks, and out-sweet your opponent.
How do I play?
Open the game in any modern browser and play free - no install, no account. 1-2 players, with gamepad, keyboard and touch controls.
Who made this?
Stephens Arcade is a family project: games designed and playtested by John Stephens and his two sons, and built with Claude. Every game ships only after it runs at 60fps on the family's Raspberry Pi arcade cabinet - and survives the seven-year-old's playtest.
