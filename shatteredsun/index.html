ABOUT THE GAME

Thirty-four years ago the Keepers shattered the sun to starve the thing living inside it - and the world has lived by lantern light ever since. Shattered Sun is a complete Zelda-style action RPG: two brothers, one lantern, six dungeons, and a campaign that runs from a guttering shrine flame to a hand-built sunrise.

Explore a moor, a haunted wood, a glass desert, a frozen coast, a drowned volcanic sea and an occupied capital. Fight bosses that teach before they test. Play it alone or drop a second hero in at any moment - it was built for a seven-year-old and his dad on the same couch.

Every line of story is written to read at seven and land at forty.

FEATURES

  • Complete story campaign: 6 dungeons, 13 main quests, 20+ side quests
  • Drop-in two-player co-op at any moment
  • Bosses with readable, learnable patterns
  • A world that answers your deeds - towns light up, characters remember
  • Full gamepad, keyboard and touch support
